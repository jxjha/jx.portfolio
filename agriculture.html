01

The squeeze

Food systems in the US and UK sit at the intersection of three pressures at once, and pulling one lever tightens the others.

Demand is rising while arable land and water are shrinking. Agriculture is both a major climate contributor and a climate victim. And a food system optimised for cheap calories has quietly traded away soil health, biodiversity and resilience. The assessment started from a blunt question: which technologies actually relieve this squeeze, and which just move the problem?

70%of the world's freshwater withdrawals go to agriculture
~⅓of all food produced is lost or wasted
≈24%of global greenhouse-gas emissions come from food systems
9.7Bpeople to feed by 2050, with today's land
33%of the planet's soils are already degraded
~11%of Earth's land surface is actually arable
