01

The idea

Amazing Amdavad was a digital-media and city-marketing venture: a love letter to Ahmedabad that also happened to be a real business.

The insight was small and honest: local businesses wanted organic visibility, and the city was a story nobody was telling well online. Build a community that loves the city, and brands follow, because that community is exactly the audience they want.

On the founding team I owned content strategy, ad production, and influencer events: three things in parallel, with the community always the point.

04

The community loop

The reason 28× compounded instead of stalling: every piece of output fed the next input. Nothing was one-and-done. This is what a growth loop actually looks like when you draw it.

Growth loop · a compounding cycle, not a funnel

Content → audience → brands → events → more content.

Step 01
Content

City-first, series-driven, high-frequency: the reason people followed in the first place.

Step 02
Audience

The follower base compounded because each piece was designed to be shared inside the city network.

Step 03
Brands

Local businesses wanted access to the community we'd built, so we could sell branded content and events without losing trust.

Step 04
Events

In-person events turned digital attention into real memory, and those events fed back the next month of content.

07

What I carried forward

My earliest ownership experience, and a product-strategy education in disguise. These models show up in every role since.

Carry 01
Build for a specific audience, not "everyone"

The city was the constraint. The constraint was the differentiator. Same principle now runs through every persona I write.

Carry 02
Growth loops beat growth hacks

A compounding loop you can name and draw is worth ten hits nobody understands. The chart above still shapes how I evaluate product bets.

Carry 03
Distribution is a product decision

What you make and where it lives can't be separated. Every content decision was also a channel decision. That lens is core to how I think about launches now.
