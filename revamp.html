The Premise

The brief was simple and brutal: take a product concept from a blank page to a defensible business case — as if it were a real venture, not a class assignment.

We chose the closet. Not because fashion is glamorous, but because the wardrobe is one of the most irrational systems people live with every day. We buy clothes we never wear, forget what we own, and still stand in front of a full wardrobe every morning convinced we have nothing to wear. ReVamp set out to fix that contradiction with data, gentle automation, and a nudge toward sustainability.

01

Ideation

Before a single feature, we sat with the tension. We reframed the frustration as a set of How Might We questions — the compass for everything that followed.

HMW · 01

How might we make getting dressed feel effortless instead of exhausting?

HMW · 02

How might we help people rediscover clothes they forgot they owned?

HMW · 03

How might we turn an overfull closet into less waste, not more guilt?

From a wide brainstorm we converged on one concept: an IoT-enabled wardrobe that quietly tracks what you own, learns your taste, reads the weather and your calendar, and hands you an outfit before you've finished your coffee — while flagging the pieces you never touch for resale or donation.

03

The Problems

Across all three, the same five frictions kept surfacing. This is the pain we had to earn the right to solve.

01
Decision fatigue every single morning
The first 20 decisions of the day are spent on something that shouldn't require any.
02
"Nothing to wear" in a full closet
People actively wear only ~20% of what they own — the rest is invisible.
03
Forgotten items = wasted money & textile waste
Unworn clothes are dead capital and a sustainability problem at once.
04
No memory of what pairs well
A good outfit discovered once is rarely repeated — there's no system for it.
05
Impulse buying of duplicates
Without visibility of the closet, people re-buy what they already have.

RICE ranked the value. MoSCoW drew the line for version one — what ships, what waits, what we deliberately refuse to build yet.

Must

  • Weather-aware outfit engine
  • Mobile app core
  • Basic taste learning
Should

  • Resale integration
  • Outfit memory
Could

  • Packing assistant
  • Brand marketplace
Won't
Not yet — on purpose
  • Full CV auto-tagging hardware
  • AR virtual try-on
  • In-app commerce

The MVP & Roadmap

Everything converged into one lean first release — then a path to grow into the full vision.

Phase 1 · Prove
The Effortless Morning

App + assisted closet capture + weather-aware outfit engine + underused-item flagging. One job, done brilliantly: get dressed faster, waste less.

Phase 2 · Retain
The Closet That Learns

Taste model deepens, outfit memory, resale loop with Vinted. The product gets smarter the more you wear it.

Phase 3 · Expand
The Connected Wardrobe

Hardware layer, brand marketplace, community. Hardware + subscription + commission — the full three-stream model.
