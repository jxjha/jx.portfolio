01

A real client, live

Most of the work in this portfolio is mine to define. This one wasn't. Vedang Yog Studio is a real client — a boutique yoga studio in Madhapar, Bhuj, run by Purvi Soni, who has taught for over a decade and holds a World Records India title. And vedangyoga.in is a real website, live on its own domain and working for the studio every day.

It's the piece I'm happiest to point at, because someone other than me decided it was good enough to ship. The brief was practical: give a local studio a home on the web that feels as calm as the practice, lays out the batches, pricing and Ayurveda clearly, and makes booking effortless — all of it in the two languages its students actually use.

So the site does a handful of quiet jobs well: set the tone, explain the offering, prove the credentials, and open a one-tap door to WhatsApp — in English and Gujarati, on whatever phone it lands on.

04

Built with Claude

The interesting part isn't only the site — it's how it got made. I designed and built it with Claude as the pair-programmer: setting the tone and structure, making the calls, and shipping a production website to a client's own domain. The decisions that make or break a small-business site — what "calm" looks like, what to leave out, how a first visit should feel — are human. The AI is the fast hands.

Under the hood it's deliberately simple and durable: one page of plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no framework and no build step, so it loads fast and can be hosted anywhere with nothing to maintain. The contact form needs no backend — it composes a WhatsApp message and hands off to the app the studio already lives in.

The bilingual layer is the neat trick: both languages share one DOM and one URL, with the Gujarati carried in data-gu attributes and swapped in place. It remembers your choice, and greets Gujarati-set browsers in Gujarati. And it's tuned for local discovery — schema.org structured data, real reviews, and keywords in English, Gujarati and Hindi.
